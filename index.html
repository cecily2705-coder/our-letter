For You, When You Miss Me ♡

My Love,

If you're reading this, I'm guessing you miss me. And honestly, I probably miss you too.

I hope you know that even when we're not together, there are so many tiny things that remind me of you. A song, a random place, something stupid that makes me laugh because I know exactly what you'd say about it. You know how I do stuff pretending you're next to me whenever I miss you, always remember that you never leave my mind.

I wish I could just magically appear beside you right now, steal your space, and cuddle with you like we always do.

Until then, consider this letter a tiny piece of me being with you.

And yes, you are allowed to miss me. But don't miss me too much. I'll be back. ♡

Love,
Cecily ♡
