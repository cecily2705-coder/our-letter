For When You Need a Reminder ♡

My baby,

Just a little reminder, because sometimes we all need one.

You are loved. A lot.

Not just for the big things you do, but for all the little things too. Your stupid jokes, your expressions, the way you hold my hand, the random things you say, and all those tiny habits that have somehow become part of my everyday life.

I hope you never feel like you have to be someone else to deserve love.

I love you — exactly as you are.

And if you ever forget that, come back and read this again.

I'll keep reminding you. ♡

Love,
Cecily ♡
