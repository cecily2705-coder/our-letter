Happy Anniversary, My Love ♡

My love,

Another year of us. ❤️

Sometimes I sit and think about how a person who was once just someone I happened to meet became such a huge part of my life. Somehow, you became the person I want to tell everything to, the person I look for in a room, the hand I automatically reach for, and the person who has found a place in so many of my ordinary days.

When I look at all the memories we've made, it's not just the big moments that I remember. It's the little things. The random conversations that went on forever. The stupid jokes that would make absolutely no sense to anyone else. And all those completely ordinary moments that I didn't realize were becoming memories until I looked back at them.

We've had our perfect days, our difficult days, our arguments, our laughter, and everything. We've seen different sides of each other, grown together, learned each other, and yet continued choosing each other.

And I think that's one of the things I love most about us.

It's not that everything is always perfect. It's that through all the imperfect moments, you're still the person I want beside me.

Thank you for loving me in all the little ways you do. Thank you for the memories, the patience, the laughter, the comfort, and for simply being you.

I don't know what every year ahead of us will look like. I don't know all the places we'll go, all the things we'll experience, or all the versions of ourselves we'll become.

But I do know that I want to keep finding out with you.

I want more ordinary days with you. More stupid arguments we'll laugh about later. More places we've never been. More memories that we don't even know we're making yet.

And if I could go back to the beginning and meet you all over again, knowing everything I know now, I would still choose you.

Every single time.

Happy anniversary, my love.

Thank you for becoming my favorite part of so many days.

Here's to everything we've been, everything we are, and everything that's still waiting for us.

I love you more than this little website could ever fit into words. ❤️

Always yours,
Cecily ♡
