For When You're Mad at Me 😣💔

My love,

If you're reading this, You're probably mad And before anything else, I want you to know that I'm not writing this to tell you that you shouldn't be angry.

If I hurt you, disappointed you, or made you feel like I wasn't listening, then you have every right to feel what you feel. I don't want to make your feelings smaller just because I wish things had gone differently.

And I know that sometimes saying “I'm sorry” isn't enough. Especially when I make the same mistake again. I know how frustrating that must be — to hear my apology and then have to deal with the same thing all over again.

I don't want you to think that because I repeat a mistake, I don't care about how it affects you. I do. Probably more than I know how to put into words.

Sometimes I don't realize how deeply something has hurt you until I see it from your side, and I'm sorry for the times I've made you feel unheard, unimportant, or taken for granted.

I'm not going to promise that I'll suddenly become perfect, because you know me well enough to know that would be a lie. But I can promise that I want to keep trying.

I want to learn from the things that hurt you. I want my apologies to become actions, not just words. And I want to become someone who makes you feel loved, not someone you constantly have to explain your hurt to.

If you're angry right now, it's okay. Take your time. You don't have to forgive me immediately. You don't even have to smile at this stupid little letter yet.

But whenever you're ready, I hope you remember one thing: I never want one of my mistakes to make you question how much you mean to me.

Because you mean so much to me. More than our silly arguments. More than the moments when we misunderstand each other. More than our ego could ever be worth.

I don't want to win an argument with you. I'd rather understand you. I'd rather fix things with you. I'd rather sit beside you after we've both calmed down, hold your hand, and find our way back to each other.

So if you're still mad at me after reading this... okay. 😭 I'll give you your space. But just know that somewhere in that space, there is a girl who is thinking about you, wishing she could take back the moment that hurt you, and hoping she gets the chance to do better.

I'm sorry for the times I've hurt you. I'm sorry for the times I've made you repeat yourself. And I'm sorry for the mistakes I haven't learned from quickly enough.

Thank you for being patient with me, even when I haven't made it easy.

I can't promise you a relationship without mistakes. But I can promise you that I will never stop caring about the person on the other side of them.

And when you're ready... come hold my hand again. ♡

Still yours,
Cecily ♡
